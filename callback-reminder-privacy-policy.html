Privacy Policy — Callback Reminder

Last updated: May 13, 2026  ·  Developer: Sreekanth  ·  Contact: sreekanth.indlas@gmail.com

Short version: Callback Reminder processes phone numbers and contact names entirely on your device to create Google Calendar reminders. We do not operate any servers, and we do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

1. What This App Does

Callback Reminder detects missed or rejected phone calls and offers to schedule a Google Calendar reminder so you remember to call back. The app runs a background service to monitor call state, shows a prompt asking when you would like to call back, and creates a calendar event with the chosen time.

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not record, store, or transmit the content of any phone call.
  • We do not read your call log history (READ_CALL_LOG permission is not requested).
  • We do not access your existing calendar events — the app only creates new events.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not operate any servers of our own. No data is ever sent to the developer.

5. Google Calendar API

This app uses the Google Calendar API to create reminder events in your Google Calendar on your behalf. The app requests only the calendar.events scope, which allows it to create, edit, and delete events. The app only creates new events — it never reads, modifies, or deletes your existing calendar data.

Your use of Google services is also governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob. If you are located in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent form before any personalised ads are loaded. You may choose to receive non-personalised ads or opt out entirely. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.

7. Firebase Crashlytics

The app uses Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash reports and diagnostic information when the app encounters an error. This data includes device model, OS version, app version, and a stack trace of the error. It does not include personal information such as your name, phone number, or account email. You can learn more at Firebase Privacy and Security.

8. Firebase Remote Config

The app uses Firebase Remote Config to allow the developer to update certain in-app configuration values (such as enabling or disabling ads) without requiring an app update. No personal data is collected through this service.

9. Data Retention

The phone number and caller name detected for a missed call are held in memory only for the duration of the reminder prompt and are discarded as soon as the app creates the calendar event or the user dismisses the prompt. The Google account email is retained in the app's private storage on your device until you sign out. Crash data retained by Firebase Crashlytics is subject to Firebase's data retention policies.

10. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this app, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
